### What to Research (Spend 30-60 minutes)

#### 1. Company Overview
**Look for:**
- Company mission and values
- What "Ikigai" means and why they chose this name
- Company history and founding story
- Size of the company (number of employees)
- Locations/offices (international presence)

**Take notes on:**
- Key phrases they use to describe themselves
- Values they emphasize
- What makes them unique

#### 2. Products & Games
**Look for:**
- What games do they develop? (Casino, slots, table games, live dealer?)
- Game portfolio/showcase
- Target audience/markets
- Technology stack mentioned
- Innovation or unique features

**Questions to consider:**
- Which games would you be most excited to work on?
- What do you notice about their visual style?
- How do they position themselves in the market?

#### 3. Company Culture
**Look for on LinkedIn:**
- Recent posts and updates
- Employee testimonials or spotlights
- Team photos or culture videos
- Office environment
- Company events or milestones

**On the website:**
- Career page descriptions
- Team page (if available)
- Benefits and perks mentioned
- Growth opportunities

**Note:**
- How they describe their work environment
- Values they emphasize (collaboration, innovation, etc.)
- Work-life balance indicators

#### 4. Recent News & Updates
**LinkedIn Activity (last 3-6 months):**
- Product launches
- Company announcements
- Industry recognition or awards
- Team growth/hiring announcements
- Partnerships or expansions

**Why this matters:**
- Shows you're genuinely interested
- Gives you conversation topics
- Helps you ask informed questions

#### 5. Team Structure
**Look for information about:**
- Engineering team structure
- Frontend vs Backend team sizes
- Remote/hybrid/in-office setup
- International distribution
- Who you might be working with

## 🎯 Follow-Up Email Best Practices

### DO:
✅ Send within 24 hours (ideally within 12 hours)  
✅ Personalize - mention specific topics discussed  
✅ Keep it concise (3-4 paragraphs max)  
✅ Proofread carefully for typos/grammar  
✅ Reiterate your interest and enthusiasm  
✅ Thank them for their time  
✅ Use a clear subject line  
✅ Match their communication style (formal vs casual)

### DON'T:
❌ Send a generic template  
❌ Write a novel (keep it short)  
❌ Be overly aggressive or pushy  
❌ Mention salary/benefits (too early)  
❌ Apologize for lack of experience  
❌ Send immediately after (wait a few hours)  
❌ Follow up multiple times if no response  
❌ Include new concerns or red flags

## 📅 Post-Interview Action Plan

### Immediately After Interview
- [ ] Write down key topics discussed while fresh in memory
- [ ] Note any promises you made (sending portfolio, code samples, etc.)
- [ ] Write down their names and roles (if multiple interviewers)
- [ ] Record any technical questions you struggled with

### Within 24 Hours
- [ ] Send personalized thank-you email
- [ ] Provide any requested materials
- [ ] Update your notes about the company

### Week 1
- [ ] If they said "we'll get back to you in X days," wait until X+2 days
- [ ] If no timeline given, wait 5-7 business days before following up
