## 💡 Key Success Tips

### 1. **Practice Out Loud**
Don't just read - actually speak your answers. Recording yourself is even better.

### 2. **Use STAR Method**
For behavioral questions:
- **S**ituation: Context (2-3 sentences)
- **T**ask: Your responsibility (1-2 sentences)
- **A**ction: What you did specifically (3-4 sentences)
- **R**esult: Outcome (2-3 sentences, quantify if possible)

### 3. **Be Authentic**
Don't memorize scripts. Use materials as guidance, but be yourself.

### 4. **Leverage Your Strengths**
- Your React expertise is solid ✓
- Your scheduling project is relevant ✓
- Your willingness to learn PixiJS/Spine is valuable ✓

### 5. **Handle Gaps Honestly**
"I haven't used PixiJS in production yet, but I've researched it and understand [core concepts]. I have strong fundamentals in [related skills] that translate well. I'm excited to dive deep and master gaming technologies."

### 6. **Ask Great Questions**
Shows interest and engagement. Prepare 5-6 questions.
